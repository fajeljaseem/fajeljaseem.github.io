What I do

A complete interactive experience is never just an app. These are the disciplines I bring together.

Interactive Applications

Touchscreen kiosks, multi-touch installations and guided experiences for exhibitions, events and museums.

Interaction with Immersive Displays

Globes, curved and panoramic walls, portrait displays and interactive floors tuned for non-standard resolutions and pixel-maps.

Hardware Integration

RFID & NFC, sensors, encoders, buttons, motors and controllers — connected reliably over serial, UDP, OSC and HTTP.

AI & Real-time Visuals

AI photobooths, camera-based transformation and real-time diffusion pipelines with GPU-accelerated image generation.

Data & Visualisation

Real-time dashboards and heatmaps that turn camera activity and footfall into clean, readable visual stories.

Kinetic & Motion

Motor-control systems with calibration, position & speed commands and reliable software-to-hardware synchronisation.

Install & Commissioning

Deployment, network setup, calibration, on-site troubleshooting, operator training and live-event support.
